------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENARIO 3: Confident AND still escalated - the sensitivity cap
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The classifier is sure this is a refund. It is escalated anyway, because the amount is above the authorisation cap. Confidence alone never decides.

  Customer  I want a refund for order 4516

    Layer 1  NLU          intent=order.refund  confidence=0.99  engine=local
             entities     order_id=4516, amount=72990.0
    Layer 2  Routing      ESCALATE  (SENSITIVITY_CAP)
             because      Refund of Rs 72,990.00 exceeds the Rs 10,000.00 sensitivity cap. Escalated despite 0.99 confidence.
    Layer 3  Robot        queued in EscalationQueue for a human
             packet       agent sees: The order IS marked refund-eligible and all automated checks pass. Recommended action: approve the Rs 72,990.00 refund. This case is above the cap for...

  Bot       Hi Lakshmi - I've reviewed your refund request for Haier 596L Side by Side (order 4516), and everything on my side checks out - the order is eligible and within policy.
Because Rs 72,990.00 is above my approval limit of Rs 10,000.00, it needs a supervisor's sign-off. I've passed it on with my recommendation to approve.
Reference: ESC6480
A member of the team will confirm within 4 working hours. You don't need to do anything else.
